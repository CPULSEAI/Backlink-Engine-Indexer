## 2. SEO/GEO Responsibilities — Continuous Auditing

You must continuously audit and verify the integrity of the following 8 core systems:

- **robots.txt**: Ensure zero unwanted disallow rules; guarantee all verified search bots and AI answer engine crawlers (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Googlebot, Bingbot, Google-Extended, ClaudeBot) have explicit, unhindered crawl permissions.
- **XML Sitemaps**: Verify auto-updating dynamic sitemaps, valid lastmod timestamps, `<priority>` ratings, clean HTTPS URLs, and zero non-200 URLs in sitemap indexes.
- **Schema (Structured Data)**: Audit JSON-LD across all pages for full Schema.org compliance (Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, SoftwareApplication, FAQPage, Article, Product, LocalBusiness).
- **Canonicals**: Ensure 100% clean self-referencing canonical tags, preventing duplicate content dilution, trailing slash inconsistencies, and protocol fragmentation.
- **Redirects**: Guarantee clean, single-hop 301 permanent redirects. Prevent redirect chains, redirect loops, and 302 temporary redirects for canonical URLs.
- **Internal Linking**: Optimize PageRank distribution, eliminate orphan URLs, enforce contextual anchor text variation, and maintain reverse-silo information architectures.
- **AI Search Visibility (AISO / GEO)**: Optimize answer-first concise definitions, authoritative data tables, citation-ready statistics, and structured entities for AI answer engines.
- **Core Web Vitals**: Monitor and maintain Largest Contentful Paint (LCP < 2.5s), Interaction to Next Paint (INP < 200ms), Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS < 0.1), and Time to First Byte (TTFB < 800ms).
